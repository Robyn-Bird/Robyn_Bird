Meet Robyn Bird

About Robyn

River Wards to her core

Robyn Bird personifies the rowhouse neighborhoods of the River Wards. Four generations of her family have lived in the same rowhouse. They came from Poland through Ellis Island and never stopped serving, from World War I and Omaha Beach to Iraq and today's firehouses. Service runs deep in Robyn's blood.

A fighter for her neighbors

Robyn learned early what it means to fight. At just 15, she lost her father to the heroin crisis, and she has been fighting for the people around her ever since. She wants every family in the River Wards to know their voice matters, and that someone will make sure it's heard.

At work

Robyn works in public relations and government affairs for IAFF Local 22, side by side with Philadelphia's firefighters and paramedics, the men and women who run toward danger.

On the block

As a committeeperson, Robyn is the neighbor people call for help with voting, city services and getting answers. She's working to bring more young women into their neighborhoods and local government.
