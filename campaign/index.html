The 2026 campaign

In 2026 Robyn ran for State Representative for the River Wards, her first run for office.

Why I ran

I ran because I love where I'm from. At door after door I met neighbors who didn't know who represents them, and families worried about whether they can afford to stay. I wanted them to have someone who actually shows up.

“I have seen Robyn's dedication firsthand. She cares about the people of this union, takes their concerns to heart and is willing to have the difficult conversations necessary to fight for us.”Mike Bresnan, President, IAFF Local 22

What I ran on

Safe streets

Back the firefighters, paramedics and police who protect us, and stand with victims, not criminals.

Schools that work

Strong neighborhood public schools, and real school choice for parents.

Neighborhoods families can afford

Protect homeowners and seniors from rising property taxes and utility costs, and grow good union jobs.

Someone who shows up

Help anyone who asks, no matter their party.

Thank you

I ran because I love where I'm from, and I still do. To every voter, neighbor, friend and donor who opened your door, took a sign, gave what you could or stopped me on the street to talk: thank you. To the firefighters and paramedics of Local 22, who had my back from day one: I'll never forget it.

I heard you at every door. You want safe streets, good schools and someone who actually shows up. That doesn't stop because an election did. I'm not going anywhere.
