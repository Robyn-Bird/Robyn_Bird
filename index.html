Who I am

River Wards to my core

Four generations of my family have lived in the same Port Richmond rowhouse. They came from Poland through Ellis Island and never stopped serving, from World War I and Omaha Beach to Iraq and today's firehouses.

I learned early what it means to fight for the people around you. That's what I do at work, and it's what I do on my block.

I'm not going anywhere. These are the neighborhoods that raised me, and I'm still showing up for them.

What I do

Work, block and community

Robyn · In the community

Committeeperson

I'm the neighbor you call when you need something from the city, a question answered about voting, or someone to show up at the civic meeting. Committeepeople are the first link between our blocks and local government, and I take that seriously.

  • Helping neighbors register, find their polling place and vote
  • Connecting people with city services and the right office
  • Civic associations, block cleanups and community events

Robyn · Local 22

Public relations & government affairs

I work alongside Philadelphia's firefighters and paramedics at IAFF Local 22, telling their story and standing up for the people who run toward danger for all of us.

  • Public relations and media for the union
  • Government affairs on the issues that affect first responders
  • Advocating for staffing, resources and respect

Robyn · Women at the table

More women involved

The decisions that shape our blocks get made close to home. I'm working to get more young women off the sidelines and into their neighborhoods and local government.

If you've been thinking about getting involved, let's do it together.

What I stand for

Commonsense, close to home

Safe streets

Back the firefighters, paramedics and police who protect us. Stand with victims, not criminals. Clean, safe blocks where kids can play outside.

Good schools

Strong neighborhood public schools, and real choice for the parents who know their kids best.

Staying put

Families who built these neighborhoods should be able to afford to stay, with fair property taxes, lower utility bills and good union jobs.

Neighbors first

Local government touches every block, every day. I'll help anyone who asks, no matter their party.
